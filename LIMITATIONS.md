# Limitations

- The theorem is exact only for values `{0,1,2}`, deterministic anonymous monotone Boolean allocation rules, normalized critical payments, integer costs, and the stated no-subsidy/build-at-all-maximum conditions. The preregistered result has three agents; n=3..6 searches are computational cross-checks.
- Subsidized DSIC mechanisms, randomized rules, Bayesian objectives, asymmetric mechanisms, continuous values, and endogenous costs are outside the search class.
- The held-out value-3 audit is a stress test of the threshold family, not a proof about all mechanisms at that magnitude.
- The independent checker is a second implementation, not an external replication or peer review.
- No novelty claim is made without a refreshed literature search and domain-expert review.
- The all-agent statement is a theorem for this narrow finite ternary class, not an asymptotic theorem over richer value domains or mechanism classes.
- The uniform-repair result covers only a single global output-bias offset in
  the declared deleted-input network form. It does not preserve or establish
  individual rationality, redistribution quality, utility bounds, or welfare;
  the large synthetic offsets demonstrate this limitation rather than solve it.
