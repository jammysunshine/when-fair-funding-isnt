# Limitations

Only two agents, two alternatives, deterministic mechanisms, unit binary values, integer transfers in `{-1,0,1}`, and unilateral deviations are covered. Uniform welfare is descriptive rather than a welfare guarantee. Anonymity is reported but not required. There is no randomization, Bayesian incentive analysis, coalition analysis, false-name analysis, continuous-domain reduction, external replication, or novelty review beyond the recorded primary sources.
