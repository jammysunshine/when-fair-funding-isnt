# Draft paper outline

## Title

Exact finite frontiers for no-deficit public-project mechanisms: a certificate-first automated design benchmark

## Abstract (calibrated)

We present a solver-free, certificate-first benchmark for deterministic public-project mechanism design. On a three-agent, three-level valuation domain, we exhaustively enumerate all anonymous monotone Boolean allocation rules and attach normalized critical payments. Requiring dominant-strategy incentive compatibility, ex-post individual rationality, anonymity, and weak budget balance yields a cost-indexed frontier: four rules survive at cost three, while accepted counts contract from four to one as cost rises from one to six. An independent checker reproduces every serialized acceptance. The efficient critical-payment rule fails budget balance, and a held-out value-magnitude audit records 207 cost-coverage failures. The result is a reproducible finite characterization and a falsification benchmark, not a continuous-domain impossibility theorem or an unqualified novelty claim.

## Positioning and result

The study operationalizes the classic efficiency/budget-balance tension and complements prior automated mechanism-design work with a compact, independently replayable certificate. Any submission would need external novelty review, larger domains, and comparison to unrestricted payment normalizations before claiming a new contribution.
