This supports the useful-artifact claim that the executable finite verifier/search pipeline is reproducible. It does not establish optimality or characterization outside its four-profile domain.

This is a rediscovery/smoke test, not independent evidence: it is evaluated by the primary verifier and has no coverage guarantee.
